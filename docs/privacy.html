form-keeper

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

form-keeper is a browser extension that saves what you type in web forms and restores it after a reload. This policy explains what it handles. The short version: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is sent to anyone.

What form-keeper stores

  • The values of form fields on pages where form-keeper is active (text you typed, boxes you ticked, options you chose), together with the page address (without its query string) and a label for each field.
  • Files you attach in upload fields, so they can be attached again after a reload. This is on for development sites. On any other site you have enabled, files are saved only if you turn on “Save attached files on other sites” in the popup's settings. Files over 8 MB are remembered by name only.
  • Your settings: which sites you enabled, which you paused, and controls you taught it.

This is stored with the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
  • It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data, and does not use it for anything other than restoring your forms.
  • It never saves password fields, hidden fields, file inputs, credit card fields (autocomplete="cc-…"), or anything marked data-form-keeper="off".

Where it runs

By default only on development addresses (localhost, 127.0.0.1, *.localhost, *.local). On any other site it runs only after you choose “Enable on this site” (or “all sites”) and your browser grants the permission. You can remove a site, or pause form-keeper on it, at any time from the popup.

Permissions

  • storage and unlimitedStorage: keep saved forms, settings and attached files in your browser, without the default size limit that attached files would quickly hit.
  • activeTab: read the address of the tab you open the popup on, to offer “Enable on this site”.
  • scripting: start working on a site right after you enable it, without a reload.
  • Optional access to http and https sites: requested only when you enable a site.

Your control

The popup lets you clear the saved data for a page or for everything. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: please open an issue.
